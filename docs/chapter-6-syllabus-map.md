## Not in scope (flag if requested)
- Dark matter / dark energy detailed models — not required by Topic 6.
- HR diagrams, luminosity classes, or quantitative stellar spectra analysis.
- Kepler’s laws by name (orbital ideas appear via speed, period and elliptical energy, not formal “laws”).
- Quasars, gravitational lensing, or general relativity.
- Exact cosmological parameters beyond the syllabus `H₀` estimate and `1/H₀` age idea.
- Naming or memorising exoplanet catalogues.
