## Not in scope (flag if requested)
- Sublimation / deposition (solid ↔ gas) — syllabus excludes these transfers.
- Quantitative latent heat (`L = E/m`) — not required in 2026–2028 0625 Topic 2.
- Ideal-gas equation with moles (`pV = nRT`) — only `pV = constant` at fixed T.
- Detailed kinetic-theory derivations of pressure formulas.
